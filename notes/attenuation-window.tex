\section*{Attenuation Window}

Assume \(0<b\le w/2\), \(x\in[0,w]\), and \(y\in[0,h]\). Let the distance
to the nearest horizontal edge be
\[
  d(x)=\min(x,\,w-x),
\]
and define the smoothstep ramp
\[
  S(u)=3u^{2}-2u^{3},\qquad u\in[0,1].
\]
Then the attenuation window is
\[
  A(x)=
  \begin{cases}
    0, & d(x)\le b,\\[4pt]
    S\!\left(\dfrac{d(x)-b}{b}\right), & b<d(x)<2b,\\[10pt]
    1, & d(x)\ge 2b,
  \end{cases}
\]
and the desired function is
\[
  \boxed{
    f(x,y,b,w,h)=y\,A(x)
  }
\]
so that \(f\) is zero on \(0\le x\le b\) and \(w-b\le x\le w\), rises
smoothly from \(0\) to \(y\) on \(b<x<2b\) and \(w-2b<x<w-b\), and equals
\(y\) in the middle region \(2b\le x\le w-2b\).

Equivalently, in compact form,
\[
  f(x,y,b,w,h)
  =
  y\left[3u^{2}-2u^{3}\right],
  \qquad
  u=\operatorname{clamp}\!\left(\frac{\min(x,w-x)-b}{b},0,1\right),
\]
where \(\operatorname{clamp}(t,0,1)=\max(0,\min(t,1))\).

Here \(h\) only fixes the vertical range \(y\in[0,h]\); it does not affect
the horizontal attenuation. For a linear rather than smooth ramp, use
\(S(u)=u\).
